# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Native Viewer with Training, Policy, Value, Network, Dynamics and Environment workspaces.
- Tabular, statistical and neural learning with explicit and learned-model planning, structured observations and conditional actions.
- Portable diagnostic recording, historical inspection, optional reference comparisons and episode playback.
- Optional residual-action critics, categorical distributional learning, critic initialization and Bellman backup choices, potential shaping, exact finite-horizon planning and BIC factored transition fitting.
- Portable Windows viewer distribution with the rocket landing demonstration and two risk configurations of the simulated alpha execution demonstration.
